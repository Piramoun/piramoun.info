About me

I'm a senior .NET developer with more than 15 years of experience building and modernizing enterprise applications in fintech, healthcare, pharmaceutical and food manufacturing, and regulated B2B environments.

Today I'm moving a legacy WPF application to Blazor and ASP.NET Core REST APIs for a Canadian financial services company. I specialize in the data side of modernization: data dictionaries, source-to-target mapping, Liquid (Scriban) templating and XML/JSON ingestion into SQL Server.

I'm known as a strong debugger who finds the root cause of complex production issues fast. I also use AI-assisted tools such as GitHub Copilot for code review, refactoring and SQL optimization.

I also led development of QcPlot, an internal quality control platform for medical laboratories.
